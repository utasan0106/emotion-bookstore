# Tokyo Pilot Cycle 01 — Neutral Recruitment Brief

Status: **READY COPY / NO OUTREACH SENT / PAID RECRUITMENT REQUIRES FOUNDER APPROVAL**

## Inclusion

- 18歳以上
- 東京に住む / 働く / 学ぶ / 月1回以上程度訪れる、のいずれか
- Tokyo Pilotを過去に操作しておらず、3 Object / Hook / Reveal や本テストの仮説を事前に聞いていない
  （該当する場合も参加は可能。`prior_pilot_exposure=yes` として記録し、primary valid n には入れない）
- 自分の普段の端末で短いWeb画面を見られる

Do not recruit specifically for culture fandom, unusual-place interest, emotion interest, or heavy social use.

## Neutral invite

> 短いWebページを自由に見ていただき、そのあといくつか感想を伺います。内容の知識や事前準備は不要です。操作と回答は匿名IDでメモし、氏名や私的な内容は研究記録に残しません。途中でやめても大丈夫です。

The invite must not tell the participant what the product is betting on. Anything in the
list below hands them the hypothesis before they look, and they will then look for it.

Never appear in recruitment, scheduling, or reminder messages:

- `3分` / `5分` / any stated duration or fixed question count
- `検索前`
- `文化との出会い`
- `3つだけがポイント` / `3つ`
- `Human Editorial`
- `また見たいかを調べています`
- `新しい発見`
- `面白い場所`
- `変わった場所`
- `東京の面白い場所`
- `開きたくなるか`
- Object名 / Hook / Reveal

Do not state a fixed number of questions either (`短い質問を4つ` etc.). V3.2 asks four
questions of everyone and a fifth only of participants who opened something, so any fixed
count is both priming and wrong.

## Recruitment quality

Aim for the first 12 primary-valid participants to be at least 2/3 `unknown` + `weak_tie`,
at most 1/3 `close_tie`. Convenience-only recruiting from close ties produces agreeable
Return Desire that does not generalise.

If the target cannot be met, keep the sample and record the shortfall as a validity caveat
in the result. Do not invalidate participants for being close ties, and do not present a
close-tie-heavy Return Desire as market demand evidence.

## Contact-data separation

Scheduling contact details must never be stored in `scorecard.local.csv`, GitHub, or the Drive canonical research summary. Research rows use P01… only.

## Compensation

No compensation is promised. Any paid panel, incentive, gift card, vendor, or recruitment contract requires Founder approval before commitment or spend.
